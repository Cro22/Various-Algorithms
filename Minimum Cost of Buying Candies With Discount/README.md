# Minimum Cost of Buying Candies With Discount
#### _Description_
A shop offers a discount: for every two candies you buy, you get a third candy for free, and the free candy must cost less than or equal to the minimum of the two purchased candies. Given an array `cost` where `cost[i]` is the price of the `i`-th candy, return the minimum total cost to buy **all** the candies. (Sort descending and take every third candy for free.)
